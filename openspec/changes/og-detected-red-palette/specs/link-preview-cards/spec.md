## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Card images
`public/og/site.png`, `verdict-detected.png`, `verdict-not_detected.png`, `verdict-uncertain.png`, `pending.png` and `unavailable.png` SHALL be 1200x630 PNGs of at most 300 KB, legible at 600x315, with the verdict label rendered as text and no confidence number, generated by the committed `scripts/og-images.py`. All cards SHALL use the ui-design-foundation colours, except `verdict-detected.png`, which SHALL use the red palette of the scenario below.

#### Scenario: Image size
- **WHEN** a test reads the PNG IHDR of each file
- **THEN** the dimensions are 1200x630 and the file size is at most 300 KB

#### Scenario: Detected card palette
- **WHEN** `verdict-detected.png` is rendered
- **THEN** its accent is `#c0392b` (`--danger`), its background is light red with a red channel above its green and blue channels and no green, and its ink text has a contrast of at least 7:1 against that background

#### Scenario: Detected generator entry
- **WHEN** a test reads `scripts/og-images.py`
- **THEN** the `verdict-detected` entry uses accent `#c0392b` and contains neither `#1f8a4c` nor `#e4f3e8`
