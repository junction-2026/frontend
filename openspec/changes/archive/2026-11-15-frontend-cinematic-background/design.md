## Decisions
- **Card placement in CSS only.** A card's `left` (odd) or `right` (even) is the lower of two values: its centre inside the outer 15% of the viewport, or its right edge plus 56px (drift and tilt) clear of the 760px composer column (`50% - min(380px, 50% - 16px)`). Percentages refer to the fixed layer, so scrollbars do not skew it. Where the gutter is too narrow (about 768px to 1100px) the card slides partly off screen instead of onto the content.
- **Opacity lives in keyframes.** `animation` overrides a static `opacity`, so small-screen dimming is applied to `.ambient-card-body`, a child of the animated `.ambient-card`.
- **Highlight sequence on one 180s cycle.** Card loops (30, 36 and 45s for the three demo cards) divide 180s, so each sequence recurs at a fixed point of its card's loop (near the opacity plateau), and the three 10s windows (starts at 6s, 76s and 133s of the cycle, applied through a negative `--seq` delay) never overlap. The highlight fill and glow are `::before`/`::after` pseudo-elements so only their `opacity` animates and the text never fades; the glow is a static `box-shadow` whose opacity animates.
- **Reduced motion hides cards** rather than freezing them over content; glows and veil stay at rest.
- **Demo text.** `text` contains the highlighted `phrase` as a substring; without a match the card renders plain (no highlight) rather than failing.
